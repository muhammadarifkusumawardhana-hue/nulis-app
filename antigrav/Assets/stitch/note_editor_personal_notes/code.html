The Mossy Path

The morning light filters through the canopy in soft, amber shafts. There is a stillness here that feels heavy, like a thick wool blanket laid across the earth.

  • Wet soil beneath boots
  • The distant call of a cedar waxwing
  • The smell of rain on stone

It's in these moments that the words come easiest. Writing doesn't feel like work; it feels like gathering stones from a creek bed.

"Nature is not a place to visit. It is home." — Gary Snyder

Every leaf turned is a sentence began.
